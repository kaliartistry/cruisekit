# CruiseKit for iPhone Is Now Available

CruiseKit is now available for iPhone, giving cruise travelers a practical way
to plan the real cost of a sailing and keep the day-by-day details organized
from one place.

The first iPhone release focuses on the parts of cruise planning that travelers
actually need before and during a trip:

- A true cost calculator for estimating gratuities, drinks, WiFi, excursions,
  port spending, and other common add-ons.
- Cruise line comparison tools for checking the tradeoffs behind the advertised
  fare.
- Port guides and itinerary planning for cruise days ashore.
- MyDay tools for schedule planning, spend tracking, and cruise-day context.
- Optional MyCrew check-ins for coordinating with people in your travel group.

CruiseKit is not a booking engine. It does not sell cruises, process
reservations, or replace the cruise line's official app. It is a planning
companion built to help cruisers understand the real total before they book and
stay organized once they are closer to sailing.

Android is still on the way. We are starting with iPhone first so CruiseKit can
get into travelers' hands sooner, while the Google Play release moves through
its required testing and review process.
