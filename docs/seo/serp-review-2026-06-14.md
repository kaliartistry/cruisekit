# CruiseKit Priority SERP Review

Date: 2026-06-14  
Scope: Public SERP snippets and pages only. This is an initial review for the keyword gate; refresh after Search Console and Keyword Planner exports are available.

## Summary

Observed ranking pages are usually one of four types:

- Standalone calculators: Cruzely, Cruise Hive, Cruise Mummy, CruiseTipsTV, cruise-tip calculators.
- Blog/how-to guides: Cruise Critic, Cruzely, NerdWallet, CruiseHabit, Finding Jillian.
- Forum/community threads: Reddit, Cruise Critic boards, Facebook public group posts.
- Official cruise-line pages: Royal Caribbean, Carnival, Norwegian, Virgin Voyages.

CruiseKit has a strong feature fit because most SERPs separate the calculator, hidden-cost, gratuity, drink-package, and line-specific answers across different sites. CruiseKit can win by making the calculator the hub and using guides/blog pages as exact-intent spokes.

## Public Question Language To Reuse

Use these as FAQ/H2/social-hook wording:

- How much should I actually budget for a 7-day cruise?
- Is the drink package worth it if I only drink a few drinks a day?
- How much cash should I bring on a cruise?
- How much cash should I bring for tips?
- What hidden fees should I expect on a cruise?
- What costs are not included in a cruise fare?
- Is NCL Free at Sea really free?
- How much are Royal Caribbean gratuities per day?
- Is Carnival CHEERS worth it after gratuity?
- How much does cruise WiFi cost?
- How much should I budget for excursions?
- Are taxes and port fees included in cruise prices?
