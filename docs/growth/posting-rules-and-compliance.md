## Operating Rules

- Reddit and Facebook are for question mining and manual participation, not automated promotion.
- No auto-posting, repeated templates, fake accounts, DMs, vote requests, or engagement manipulation.
- No affiliate links in communities unless a community explicitly allows them and the user approves the exact post.
- Disclose affiliation when mentioning CruiseKit.
- Do not use AI-generated comments where a community bans AI content.
- Do not join private groups, scrape private data, or collect personal data.
- Use public question patterns to improve pages, FAQs, helper text, and short-form hooks.

## Community-Specific Notes

- r/Cruise: visible rules and wiki indicate strict spam and self-promotion limits. Treat as question mining and no-link manual participation only.
- r/royalcaribbean: visible rules prohibit self-promotion and monetary-benefit links. Do not link CruiseKit unless explicitly allowed and approved.
- r/CarnivalCruiseFans: visible public results show restrictive advertising/self-promotion posture. Treat as question mining until live rules are captured.
- r/travel: visible rules prohibit self-promotion, blogspam, and market-research style posts. Do not use for CruiseKit promotion.
- Facebook cruise groups: public snippets frequently show no self-promotion, no affiliate links, no travel-agent promotion, and no selling. Treat as research-only unless live rules and user approval permit a specific action.

## Draft Review Checklist

Before any post or comment:

- Is this manual and non-repetitive?
- Does the community allow the type of participation?
- Is there a clear user benefit without a promotional ask?
- Is CruiseKit affiliation disclosed if mentioned?
- Are affiliate links absent?
- Is there no private group data or personal data?
- Has the user approved the exact text and location?
