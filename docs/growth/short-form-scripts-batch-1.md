# Short-Form Scripts - Batch 1

Status: draft only. Do not post without approval.

## Script 1 - Fare Vs Real Total

Hook: That $499 cruise is not a $499 vacation.

Body:

- Start with the fare.
- Add taxes and port fees.
- Add gratuities.
- Add drinks, WiFi, excursions, parking, hotel, insurance, and port cash.
- Now compare the real total, not the screenshot.

CTA: Calculate your real cruise cost before you book.

## Script 2 - Drink Package Break-Even

Hook: The drink package is not worth it until this number.

Body:

- Take the daily package price.
- Add service charge.
- Multiply by every adult who has to buy it.
- Split sea days and port days.
- Compare that to what you would actually drink.

CTA: Run the drink package math before checkout.

## Script 3 - Carnival CHEERS

Hook: Carnival CHEERS has one rule that changes everything.

Body:

- If one adult in the cabin buys it, the cabin rule can make everyone buy it.
- So do not calculate one person's drinks.
- Calculate the average across the cabin.
- That is the number that decides whether CHEERS is worth it.

CTA: Estimate your Carnival total with CHEERS included.

## Script 4 - NCL Free At Sea

Hook: NCL Free at Sea is not zero dollars.

Body:

- The perks can be valuable.
- But beverage gratuities, WiFi limits, upgrades, and excursion gaps still matter.
- Treat it like a bundle.
- Compare the bundle total against a la carte cruise lines.

CTA: Price the Norwegian cruise with the perks included.

## Script 5 - Disney Family Budget

Hook: Disney cruise budgeting starts with the family total.

Body:

- Do not stop at the per-person fare.
- Add gratuities for every guest.
- Add WiFi devices.
- Add adult dining, excursions, photos, transportation, and port spending.
- Then compare the real family total.

CTA: Calculate the Disney cruise budget before booking.

## Script 6 - Cruise WiFi

Hook: Cruise WiFi gets expensive when you multiply it.

Body:

- One phone for messaging is one budget.
- Two phones and a laptop is another.
- Streaming or work calls can push you into a higher tier.
- Multiply by devices and nights before you book.

CTA: Add WiFi to the cruise calculator.

## Script 7 - How Much Cash

Hook: Your ship account is cashless. The port is not always cashless.

Body:

- Bring small bills for taxis.
- Bring tips for guides and port help.
- Bring backup cash for small vendors.
- Keep onboard spending separate from port-day cash.

CTA: Budget port spending before the cruise.

## Script 8 - Hidden Cruise Fees

Hook: The surprise is not one fee. It is the stack.

Body:

- Taxes.
- Port fees.
- Gratuities.
- Drinks.
- WiFi.
- Excursions.
- Parking, hotel, insurance, and port cash.
- The stack is the real cruise cost.

CTA: Check the hidden-fee list before booking.

## Script 9 - 7-Day Cruise Cost

Hook: A 7-day cruise budget should not be one number.

Body:

- Build it by category.
- Fare.
- Mandatory fees.
- Daily charges.
- Per-port spending.
- Per-person add-ons.
- Then multiply guests and nights.

CTA: Run the real total before final payment.

## Script 10 - Shareable Estimate

Hook: Before the group says yes to a cruise, send the real total.

Body:

- Put the fare into the calculator.
- Add the extras everyone expects.
- Share the estimate.
- Now the group is deciding on the real trip, not just the advertised fare.

CTA: Calculate it, share it, then book.
