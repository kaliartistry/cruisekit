# CruiseKit Concierge Distribution Pilot

Status: draft only. No outreach has been sent. Kali approval is required for every recipient and message.

## Offer

CruiseKit supplies a free, attributed calculator link that an advisor or creator can share when it genuinely helps a traveler estimate the full cruise cost. The traveler can save the result and continue in CruiseKit. The distributor receives only aggregate weekly counts; CruiseKit does not disclose identities, spending, cabins, invite codes, or private sailing data.

## Unique link pattern

`https://cruisekit.app/calculator?source_type=advisor&source_id=TARGET_ID&utm_source=advisor&utm_medium=organic_partner&utm_campaign=concierge_pilot&utm_content=TARGET_ID`

Creator links use `source_type=creator` and `utm_source=creator`. IDs must be lowercase, short, and internal; they are not public partnership claims.

## Client handoff message

> I included a free independent CruiseKit link for your sailing. It helps you estimate gratuities, drinks, WiFi, excursions, and port spending, then save the cruise for MyDay and onboard spend tracking. CruiseKit is separate from the cruise line, so continue using the official app for check-in, reservations, and cruise-line updates.

## Success gate

- At least three participating distributors.
- At least ten qualified calculator completions.
- At least three saved cruises.
- At least one distributor voluntarily reuses the link for another booking or piece of content.

Do not build an advisor dashboard or public co-branding until this gate is met.
