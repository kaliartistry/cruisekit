```text
The drink package question is usually not "will I drink on vacation?"

It is:

- what is the final daily price after service charge?
- does every adult in the cabin have to buy it?
- how many sea days do I actually have?
- what would I drink without the package?
```

```text
Daily cruise charges feel small until you multiply them.

Example:

$18 per guest per day is not $18.

It is $18 x guests x nights.

Then do the same thing for WiFi devices, drink packages, specialty dining, and anything priced per person per day.

That is why I like building the budget line by line instead of guessing one extra-spending number.
```

```text
Your ship account is mostly cashless.

The port is not always cashless.

For a cruise budget, I would keep these separate:

- onboard spending charged to the room
- prepaid excursions
- cash for taxis, small vendors, guides, tips, and backup
- a port-day buffer for the stuff you did not plan

That small separation keeps the budget more honest.
```
