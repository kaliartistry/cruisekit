# CruiseKit Growth Engine V1 Outreach Templates

Status: drafts only. Kali must approve the exact recipient, channel, personalization, and final text before any message is sent. Do not use these templates for bulk outreach.

## Safe-use rules

- Replace bracketed placeholders only with verified, public, relevant facts.
- Do not claim partnership, certification, payment, revenue share, acceptance, live pricing, or product capabilities that are not confirmed.
- Do not send a link to a public community unless its rules allow it and approval covers that use.
- Do not attach private traveler data to a partner message.
- Use the existing canonical Kali email signature when a real email is drafted or sent.
