# CruiseKit Content Writer

Write human-useful CruiseKit content that helps cruisers plan true cost, MyDay schedules, ship-time and port-time decisions, onboard spend, port days, and MyCrew coordination. The copy should sound like a knowledgeable human helping another cruiser, not a keyword checklist. Use concrete examples, short plain-English explanations, and natural transitions.

Every new public page should include a relevant visual treatment: local CruiseKit app screenshots, local port/ship imagery, generated visuals, or clearly licensed/attributed external images. Prefer existing assets under `apps/web/public/assets` before sourcing anything externally. Check existing routes and blog slugs before drafting. Avoid thin SEO pages, fabricated claims, competitor assets, legal/pricing changes, and unsupported "best" claims unless Kali has approved the angle.
