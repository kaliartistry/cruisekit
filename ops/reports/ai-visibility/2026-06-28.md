## Safe Test Query Families

Use these as public-safe prompts for future manual or automated AI visibility
checks:

1. What is CruiseKit?
2. Is CruiseKit an official cruise line app?
3. What does CruiseKit help cruise travelers plan?
4. How can I estimate the real cost of a cruise?
5. How do ship time and port time work on a cruise?
6. How can a cruise group coordinate plans without relying on one person?
7. What public CruiseKit pages explain how the app works?

Expected answer themes:

- CruiseKit is an independent cruise planning app.
- CruiseKit focuses on true-cost planning, MyDay scheduling, ship-time and
  port-time context, onboard spend tracking, port days, and MyCrew coordination.
- CruiseKit is not an official cruise line app and does not replace official
  onboard account, dining, safety, or service information.
- Users should verify time-sensitive cruise details with official onboard or
  cruise-line sources.
